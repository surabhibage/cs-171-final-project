##### Logistic Regression #####

Loss: cross-entropy (log loss) with L2 regularization, 
C = 10, balanced class weights.

Optimizer was lbfgs with max_iter = 2000 and every C iterated through 
had converged well before the limit (~90 iterations at C = 10).


##### Random Forest #####

Loss: There was no gradient-trained loss. Each tree picks 
splits that minimize Gini impurity (weighted by balanced_subsample class weights), 
and the forest predicts by majority vote across the 200 trees.

##### RNN #####

Loss: cross-entropy with balanced class weights with Adam optimizer. 
The RNN ran with a learning rate at 0.001 and hidden size being 128,
with each batch size being 256, all over 20 epochs.

Training curves: The training loss had decreased steadily across all 20 epochs (about 1.87 -> 1.60), and
validation loss also decreased early on from about 1.88 to 1.70, but mostly 
leveled off over the last ~6 epochs, staying around 1.70-1.72. 
The gap between training and validation loss additionally grew from 
about 0.01 to 0.10, which shows mild overfitting starting near the end. The validation
loss never clearly rose, so 20 epochs was a reasonable stopping point, and training longer
would likely give little improvement on validation.
