##### Logistic Regression #####

Final model: 13 audio features 
(key and time_signature were one-hot encoded), C = 10 with balanced class weights.

Validation: accuracy 36.01%, macro F1: 0.289
Test: accuracy 37.04%, macro F1: 0.300, weighted F1: 0.38

++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++

Best genres (test F1): chill_jazz_classical 0.54, electronic_dance 0.54, rock_metal 0.50,latin 0.45
Worst genres (test F1): pop 0.10, world 0.10, hiphop_rnb 0.15, reggae 0.15

Class weights had traded accuracy for a better macro F1 (validation: 41.78% -> 36.01% accuracy, 0.252 -> 0.289 macro F1), 
while recall on the small genres had a large gain (reggae 0.01 -> 0.49, indie_folk 0.01 -> 0.39, country 0.20 -> 0.53). 
owever, their precision stayed low (reggae 0.09 on test), 
so it means the model had over-predicted them.

From this, both pop and world stayed weak either way, most likely because 
their audio features have some overlap with other genres.


##### Random Forest #####

Final model: 10 audio features, 200 trees, min_samples_leaf = 1, max_features = 0.5,
balanced_subsample class weights

Validation: accuracy 56%, macro F1: 0.47
Test: accuracy 57%, macro F1: 0.48, weighted F1: 0.56

Best genres (test F1): electronic_dance 0.69, chill_jazz_classical 0.67, rock_metal 0.62, latin 0.59
Worst genres (test F1): reggae 0.26, indie_folk 0.30, world 0.34, hiphop_rnb 0.40

Note: only world stayed weak here, not alongside pop

++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++

Class weights helped only slightly: with the same settings, validation macro F1 was 0.465
unweighted vs 0.474 balanced. Small genres still have low recall (test: reggae 0.18,
indie_folk 0.22), but their precision is much higher than in logistic regression (reggae
0.45 vs 0.09), so the forest's predictions of small genres are more often correct.


##### RNN #####

The final model had 13 audio features at hidden size 128 and lr 0.001,
over 20 epochs with balanced class weights.

Validation: accuracy 41%, macro F1: 0.35
Test: accuracy 42%, macro F1: 0.35, weighted F1: 0.44

++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++

Best genres (test F1): chill_jazz_classical 0.61, electronic_dance 0.61, rock_metal 0.52, latin 0.47
Worst genres (test F1): reggae 0.18, indie_folk 0.19, world 0.21, pop 0.22

Like logistic regression, the class weights had made the RNN 
over-predict small genres (high recall but low precision, and 
for the test set, country 0.68 recall / 0.21 precision, reggae 0.57 / 0.11).
Similarly to the other models as well (such as lr), the genres pop and world stayed weak.
